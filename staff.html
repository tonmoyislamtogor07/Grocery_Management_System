  • A Manager can create only Cashier accounts — never a Manager or Owner account.
  • Accounts are deactivated rather than deleted, so old bills keep the real name on the audit trail.
  • Permanent delete and role change stay with the Owner.
  • 5 wrong passwords in a row lock that login for 15 minutes — a correct password is refused until the lock clears. Owner or Manager can unlock it here.
  • Every hire, edit, deactivate, unlock and delete is logged below with who did it and when.
  • Password changes need approval: a Cashier's new password is approved by the Manager or Owner, a Manager's by the Owner, and the Owner's changes instantly.
