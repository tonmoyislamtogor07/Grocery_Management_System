OwnerYesYesYes (permanent)
ManagerYesYes (cashier only)No (deactivate only)
  • A Manager can create only Cashier accounts — never a Manager or Owner account.
  • Accounts are deactivated rather than deleted, so old bills keep the real name on the audit trail.
  • Permanent delete and role change stay with the Owner.
  • 5 wrong passwords in a row lock that login for 15 minutes — a correct password is refused until the lock clears. Owner or Manager can unlock it here.
  • Every hire, edit, deactivate, unlock and delete is logged below with who did it and when.
  • Password changes apply instantly for every role — no approval is needed. A new password must be 8–72 characters and mix an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
  • A password an Owner or Manager sets here is temporary: that person must choose their own password once, on My Account, before the rest of the system unlocks — so nobody can work under another person's name.
  • Everyone can change their own email and phone on My Account at any time; without an email, “Forgot password?” has nowhere to send a code.
